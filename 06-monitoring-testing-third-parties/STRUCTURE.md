## Conventions
`NN.NN` numbering in execution order; a metadata table at the head of every document; a `## Cross-References` table and footer navigation at the foot. Document IDs run `MRG-PCI-2026-601` through `-613`. ADRs are numbered continuously across the repository; Phase 06 contributes **0020–0025** (0023–0025 are recorded inline in the numbered documents; 0020–0022 have their own files).

The keystone deliverable is **06.09** — 11.6.1 payment-page tamper detection, which completes the script control 6.4.3 began in Phase 04 and is the reason **R-01 finally moves from High 20 to Moderate 10**. **06.08** carries the second of the programme's two Critical penetration test findings, **06.05** carries the second of its two Not in Place findings, and **06.10** is where the bill for Phase 02's 88.2% scope reduction arrives as third-party assurance work.

The Excel trackers are **generated by parsing the narrative documents** — the application findings from 06.08, the ASV quarters from 06.06, the provider register from 06.10, and the risk movements from 06.12 — with assertions verifying 11 contiguous findings at 1 Critical / 3 High / 4 Medium / 3 Low, that **Q1 failed and Q2–Q4 passed**, and that six providers are registered. A workbook cannot drift from the prose it summarises.
