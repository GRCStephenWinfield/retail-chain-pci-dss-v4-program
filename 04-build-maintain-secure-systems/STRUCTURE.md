## Conventions
`NN.NN` numbering in execution order; a metadata table at the head of every document; a `## Cross-References` table and footer navigation at the foot. Document IDs run `MRG-PCI-2026-401` through `-413`. ADRs are numbered continuously across the repository; Phase 04 contributes **0010–0012**.

The keystone deliverable is **04.10** — the 6.4.3 payment-page script control, the requirement where a merchant with a hosted checkout iframe still carries real exposure, and where the phase's finding sits. **04.08**'s significant-change definition is the quiet load-bearing one: five separate requirements hang off it, and Phase 03 demonstrated what its absence costs.

The Excel trackers are **generated by parsing the narrative documents** — the script inventory from 04.10, the configuration controls and baselines from 04.01, the significant-change triggers from 04.08, and the traceability from 04.12 — with assertions verifying 38 scripts with contiguous IDs SCR-01 to SCR-38, exactly 11 third-party, and exactly 3 recorded as unauthorised at first inventory. A workbook cannot drift from the prose it summarises.
