## Conventions
`NN.NN` numbering in execution order; a metadata table at the head of every document; a `## Cross-References` table and footer navigation at the foot. Document IDs run `MRG-PCI-2026-101` through `-113`. ADRs are numbered continuously across the repository, starting at **0001** in this phase. Mermaid diagrams throughout; Excel is colour-coded and filterable.

The keystone deliverables are **01.05** (the requirements map, including the defined-versus-customized approach), **01.08** (the scope hypothesis and the twelve assumptions written so they can be disproved), and **01.11** (the obligations calendar, which becomes the continuous compliance model in Phase 09).

The Excel trackers are **generated by parsing the narrative documents** — the requirement map from 01.05, the TPSP register from 01.09, the calendar from 01.11, and the assumptions, constraints and stakeholders from 01.08 and 01.10 — with assertions on every count, so a workbook cannot drift from the prose it summarises.
