# ADR-0003 — One Firm as Both QSA and ASV, With the Independence Position Documented

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Status | **Accepted** |
| Date | 2026-02-02 |
| Deciders | Naomi Bhatt (CISO), Rosa Delgado (Internal Audit), Raymond Voss (CFO) |

## Context

Sable Ridge Assurance is a QSA company; Sable Ridge Scanning Services is an Approved Scanning Vendor. They are the same firm, operating as separate practices. Council rules permit a single firm to hold both qualifications and to serve the same client.

Convenience is real — one relationship, one onboarding, aligned schedules. So is the question a sceptical reader will ask: could quarterly scan findings be handled gently because the same firm is assessing the ROC?

## Decision

Engage Sable Ridge for both, and **document the independence position rather than leaving it implicit.** Record the specific threats, the specific safeguards, and a date at which the arrangement is re-examined.

## Rationale

The threat is not that the firm is dishonest; it is that the arrangement is never examined. Writing it down converts an unexamined convenience into a decision someone can challenge.

The strongest safeguard turned out to be structural rather than procedural: **the ASV attestation is issued to Cardinal Merchant Bank independently of Marketa.** The Q1 2026 scan failed, was reported as failed to the acquirer, and was rescanned clean eleven days later. The failure is the evidence that the arrangement works.

## Consequences

| Safeguard | Position |
|---|---|
| Separate personnel and reporting lines | Confirmed in the engagement letters |
| ASV attestation issued independently to the acquirer | Structural, not discretionary |
| Advisory work | **Expressly excluded** from both engagement letters |
| Familiarity threat | **Assessor rotation reviewed at the 2028 cycle** |
| Internal Audit view | Delgado reviews the arrangement annually and reports to the Audit Committee |

## Alternatives considered

| Option | Why rejected |
|---|---|
| Separate firms for QSA and ASV | Cleanest on paper. Rejected on scheduling and cost for the 2026 cycle, with rotation deferred to 2028 rather than dismissed |
| Same firm, no documented position | The status quo of prior years, and the thing this ADR exists to end |
| Internal ASV | Not permitted; external scans for validation must come from an Approved Scanning Vendor |
