## 1. What the QSA does

| The QSA does | The QSA does not |
|---|---|
| Determine whether each applicable requirement is **In Place**, **In Place with Compensating Control**, **Not Applicable**, **Not Tested**, or **Not in Place** | Choose Marketa's controls, design them, or implement them |
| Select samples and define testing procedures, including derived procedures for any customized approach | Make Marketa compliant. Only the controls do that |
| Examine evidence, observe processes and interview personnel | Accept management assertion in place of evidence |
| Sign the ROC | Sign the AOC on Marketa's behalf — the assessed entity attests |

**The principle the programme operates on:** the QSA assesses; **the merchant remains responsible.** An assessor's opinion is not a transfer of accountability, and no finding of In Place obliges a card brand or the acquirer to agree.

## 2. Independence position

Sable Ridge Assurance provides the QSA service; **Sable Ridge Scanning Services** provides ASV scanning as a separate practice. The Council permits a single firm to hold both qualifications. Marketa documented the arrangement rather than assuming it was unremarkable:

| Consideration | Position |
|---|---|
| Are the practices separate? | Separate personnel, separate engagement letters, separate reporting lines within the firm |
| Could ASV findings be softened to help the ROC? | The ASV attestation is issued to Cardinal independently; **the Q1 scan failed and was reported as failed** |
| Familiarity threat over successive years | **Assessor rotation to be reviewed at the 2028 cycle** — recorded in ADR-0003 |
| Does Sable Ridge advise on control design? | **No.** Advisory work is expressly out of scope of the engagement letter |
