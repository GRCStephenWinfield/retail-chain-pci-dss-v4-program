## 1. What the Committee was told

Marketa accepts roughly **68.4 million card transactions a year across three channels** and is a **Level 1 merchant**. Validation requires an annual on-site assessment by a Qualified Security Assessor, producing a Report on Compliance and an Attestation of Compliance filed with Cardinal Merchant Bank by **31 December**.

The 2026 assessment is the organisation's **first under PCI DSS v4.0.1**, and the first in which all 51 future-dated requirements are mandatory.

## 2. What the Committee was explicitly told NOT to conclude

At the Chair's request, the following were minuted verbatim:

| Statement | Recorded |
|---|---|
| PCI DSS is **contractual, not statutory** | It reaches Marketa through the Cardinal merchant agreement. It is not a law and no regulator enforces it |
| The PCI Security Standards Council **does not enforce** | It writes the standard. Enforcement runs card brand → acquirer → merchant |
| There is **no PCI certification for a merchant** | A ROC and an AOC describe a point in time. Neither is a certificate |
| An AOC is **not** a determination of compliance with any law | And it does not immunise Marketa against state data-breach statutes or contractual liability |
| A compliant assessment **does not mean secure** | It means the assessed requirements were met on the assessed dates for the assessed scope |

## 3. The Chair's questions

| Question | Answer given |
|---|---|
| What is the worst realistic outcome? | A compromise of account data, followed by a forensic investigation, brand assessments passed through by the acquirer, card reissuance costs, and litigation. The financial exposure is not the assessment fee — it is the breach |
| Is the scope figure reliable? | **No, and it is not presented as reliable.** 604 systems is a hypothesis. Phase 02 tests it and it will move in both directions |
| Does Internal Audit have unimpeded access? | Delgado confirmed, with the CISO present, that no scope limitation has been placed on her review |
| What would you like from this Committee? | Air cover when an honest finding is unwelcome, and acceptance that a finding disclosed early is cheaper than one found at fieldwork |

## 4. Committee determinations

1. The programme mandate and the Level 1 validation path are **noted and endorsed**.
2. The Committee will receive **quarterly** reporting, with an exception route to the Chair between meetings.
3. Internal Audit's independence is **confirmed** for the engagement.
4. The Committee recorded that it does **not** wish to receive a report in which the scope figure has never moved.
