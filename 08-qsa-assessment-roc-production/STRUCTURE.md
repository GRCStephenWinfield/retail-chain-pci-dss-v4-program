## Reading order

Start at `08.00-README.md`. The numbered documents run in execution order: planning and sampling (08.01–08.02), evidence and fieldwork (08.03–08.04), the dispositions themselves (08.05–08.08), the report and attestation (08.09–08.11), remediation and re-assessment (08.12), and the register and close (08.13–08.14).

## How the trackers are produced

Every workbook is **generated by parsing the narrative markdown** with assertions on the counts — the disposition table must sum to 306, the sampling table must contain eighteen contiguous populations, each risk movement's likelihood × impact must equal its stated score, and no impact score may fall. **A workbook cannot drift from the prose, because it is derived from it.**
