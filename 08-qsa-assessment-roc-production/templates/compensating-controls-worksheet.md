## 1. Constraints

*List the legitimate technical or documented business constraints precluding compliance with the stated requirement. **"It is expensive" and "the vendor has not built it" are different statements; only one of them is a constraint.***

## 2. Objective

*Define the objective of the original control. Identify the objective the compensating control meets.*

## 3. Identified Risk

*Identify any additional risk posed by the absence of the original control.*

## 4. Definition of Compensating Controls

*Define the compensating controls and explain how they address the objectives of the original control and the increased risk, if any.*

## 5. Validation of Compensating Controls

*Define how the compensating controls were validated and tested. **Validation performed by the entity is a starting point, not a conclusion.***

## 6. Maintenance

*Define the process and controls in place to maintain the compensating controls.*

## The four tests, applied before the worksheet is drafted

| Test | Answer | If no |
|---|---|---|
| Does it meet the **intent and rigour** of the original requirement? | | Stop. This is a finding |
| Does it **address the risk the original requirement addresses**? | | Stop. This is a finding |
| Does it go **above and beyond** other PCI DSS requirements? | | Stop — you are citing a control you already owe |
| Is it **commensurate with the additional risk** imposed by not meeting the requirement? | | Stop |

**Marketa argued five and withdrew two.** The two that failed — 8.6.2 and 11.3.1.2 — failed on the second and third tests, and became Not in Place findings rather than weaker worksheets.
