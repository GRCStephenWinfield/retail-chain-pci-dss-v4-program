## 1. The rule, stated to reviewers before the draft was circulated

> A factual error is a statement about Marketa that is **wrong** — a count, a date, a system name, an owner, a described process. A characterisation is the assessor's **judgement** about what a fact means. **You may correct the first. You may not touch the second.** If you believe a characterisation is unfair, say so to the assessor and accept the answer.

## 3. The comment that was withdrawn before it was sent

The General Counsel proposed softening the assessor's description of the 8.6.2 finding. The proposal was raised in the review meeting and **withdrawn in the same meeting** on the CISO's objection:

> "The sentence is accurate. If we ask him to change it and he declines, we have created a record of Marketa trying to soften a finding. If he agrees, we have created something worse."

Recorded here because a review record that shows only the comments that were sent is a record of a process nobody tested.

## 4. What a factual review cannot do

An entity's review does not make the report a joint document. **The conclusions, the dispositions and the language are the assessor's**, and a ROC an entity has edited for tone is a document about the entity rather than an assessment of it.
