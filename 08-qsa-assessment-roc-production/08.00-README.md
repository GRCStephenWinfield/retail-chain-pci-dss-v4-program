## What this phase is

Seven phases built an environment. **This is the one where somebody else grades it.**

Sable Ridge Assurance performs fifteen days of on-site fieldwork from **2026-11-02 to 2026-11-20**, dispositions every one of the **306** applicable sub-requirements, produces a **741-page Report on Compliance**, and issues an **Attestation of Compliance on 2026-12-11 recording Marketa as non-compliant**. Both findings are remediated by **2027-01-29** and **2027-01-30**, and a re-assessment on **2027-02-18** produces a revised report showing full compliance.

The phase also does the thing most portfolios skip: it shows the **mechanics** — how a sample is chosen and by whom, what evidence can and cannot prove, how a compensating controls worksheet is actually written, what "Not Applicable" means and what it does not, and what happens when the assessor disagrees with you.

## The sentence this phase exists to earn

> **Neither of the two findings was discovered by the assessor. Both were on Marketa's register before he arrived, with dates, and in both cases a compensating controls worksheet was drafted and withdrawn rather than argued.**

8.6.2 was identified in **Phase 05**, in June. 11.3.1.2 was identified in **Phase 06**, in July. The assessor confirmed them. That is a different report from one in which an assessor finds two things an entity did not know about itself — and it is the return on eleven months of refusing to reframe inconvenient results.

## Where the non-compliance actually comes from

**Three of the five recorded exceptions trace to the same nine devices and the same support agreement.**

| Exception | Sub-req | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| **Not in Place** | 11.3.1.2 | No credential release, no agent installation rights |
| Compensating control **CCW-01** | 8.3.6 | The built-in administrative account's credential field is capped at 10 characters by firmware |
| Compensating control **CCW-02** | 2.2.7 | No management protocol offering strong cryptography |
| *(and)* the only population the assessor **could not sample** | S-12 | Standardisation could not be demonstrated |

One commercial decision, made years before the programme started, produced more of Marketa's assessed exceptions than every control gap in the estate combined. **Not compliance failure — a contract nobody had read as a security control.**

And when it was remediated, it was remediated by renegotiation: the amendment executed **2027-01-08** granted credential release, not firmware change. **CCW-01 and CCW-02 both survive the re-assessment.** The remediation removed the finding, not the constraint — which is why Route B, replacing the appliance class outright, stays on the 2027 roadmap for a finding that is already closed.

## Four determinations where the assessor and Marketa disagreed

**One went Marketa's way and three did not**, and the phase reports both kinds. A determination log in which the entity wins most of the arguments is a log kept by the entity.

| # | Item | Marketa's position | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-1 | **5.3.3** removable media | Proposed **Not Applicable** — the policy prohibits removable media | **Rejected.** *A policy prohibition is not a technical absence*, and USB ports remained enabled on **31 of 71** components. Returned to the population and assessed **In Place** |
| D-2 | **3.5.1.2 · 3.5.1.3** | Proposed **Not Applicable** — disk encryption is not the 3.5.1 mechanism | **Refused.** *Absence of use is not absence of the thing.* Both were tested and assessed **In Place** — a stronger disposition than the one Marketa argued for |
| D-3 | **10.4.2** | 11 components reviewed monthly under a targeted risk analysis | **Held.** The assessor tested **TRA-10.4.2.1's reasoning**, not its conclusion |
| D-4 | **1.2.8** | In Place across all nine zones | **Marketa was wrong.** Two of nine ruleset backups sat on an unrestricted share. Corrected 2026-11-05 and **disclosed in the report** |

Seven entries move and four are held explicitly. The register's last two High entries — **R-17** (8.6.2) and **R-36** (11.3.1.2) — come down when the requirements holding them are remediated and re-tested on **2027-02-18**. **They come down to Moderate 8, not to Low.** With impact held at 4, a Low rating would require a likelihood of 1 — the event not reasonably foreseeable — which neither entry can support.

**06.12 §7 forecast both reaching Low, and that forecast was wrong.** R-17 and R-36 are still on the register as Moderate entries at the Phase 09 close. **08.13 §6.3 goes further and declines to reconcile the published close of 0 · 13 · 31 at all**: four of the thirteen entries 03.12 §6 moves to Low reached it in Phase 06, and several of the remaining nine are scored **3 × 4** — and a 3 × 4 entry moving on likelihood alone reaches **2 × 4 = 8**, which is Moderate. **On the arithmetic visible from here the forecast is unlikely to be met**, and Phase 09 reports the actual close against it whichever way it falls.

No entry has been closed or removed in twelve months, and no High risk has ever been accepted. **Every movement in Phase 08 is a likelihood movement** — and the programme has reduced an impact score **three times** in twelve months, R-10 in Phase 06 and R-14 and R-31 in Phase 07, each named in the phase that made it rather than claimed away.

**Six proposed movements were argued and refused**, including R-02, whose case was the strongest available: four full inspection cycles, zero confirmed tampering, and 96 terminals personally observed by the assessor. It stays at Moderate 10, because the only movement available would take it to Low and *a skimmer overlay on a terminal in a public space is foreseeable — it is what the inspection programme exists for.*

## The honest position

The Attestation filed with Cardinal Merchant Bank on **2026-12-11** records Marketa as **non-compliant**. That is the correct description of an environment that did not meet two requirements on that date, and **ADR-0032** keeps it: the revised attestation of 2027-02-18 is additive, and the 2026 record stands.

Three things this phase does not claim:

**That the remediated state has an operating history.** Eighteen days separate the last remediation from the re-assessment. The state was **verified**, not observed over a period, and there was **no float** in the plan — a slip on either side would have returned a requirement Not in Place a second time.

**That compliance is now a property Marketa has.** A ROC and an AOC are a point-in-time attestation about a defined scope over a defined period. The three compensating controls must be re-argued annually and none has a vendor roadmap behind it; the customized approach at 8.3.9 carries **no forward assurance** and will be re-argued from scratch when the assessor rotates in 2028.

**That the seventeen observations are closed.** They are open, unassigned, and Phase 09's inheritance.

**PCI DSS is contractual, not statutory.** The Council writes the standard and does not enforce it. There is **no PCI DSS certification for a merchant**; no attestation is a determination of compliance with any law; and a QSA's opinion binds neither an acquirer nor a card brand. **No fine, fee schedule or penalty amount appears anywhere in this programme** — the professional fees that do appear are fees.
