## Conventions
`NN.NN` numbering in execution order; a metadata table at the head of every document; a `## Cross-References` table and footer navigation at the foot. Document IDs run `MRG-PCI-2026-701` through `-713`. ADRs are numbered continuously across the repository; Phase 07 contributes **0026–0028**.

The keystone deliverables are **07.03** (all fourteen targeted risk analyses consolidated into one register for the first time), **07.06** (the 12.5.2 annual scope confirmation — the requirement Phase 02 exists to satisfy, and the document the QSA will open first), **07.11** (12.10.7, and the third limb entities skip), and **07.07 / 07.12** (the register's last movement before assessment, on exit criteria published six months in advance).

The Excel trackers are **generated by parsing the narrative documents** — the fourteen analyses from 07.03, the procedures from 07.11, the movements from 07.12, and the policy suite from 07.02 — with assertions verifying fourteen contiguous TRA identities, exactly four 12.10.7 class procedures, exactly five risk movements, and fourteen topic-specific policies. A workbook cannot drift from the prose it summarises.
