# Information Security Policy — Approval Record

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Approval | The overarching information security policy and the topic-specific policy suite |
| Date | 2026-03-12 |
| Approved by | PCI Steering Committee, with Board noting |
| Chair | Raymond Voss, Chief Financial Officer |
| Owner | Naomi Bhatt, CISO |

## 1. What was approved

One overarching information security policy, **fourteen topic-specific policies**, three programme-level policies, and **61 operational procedures** — the set that discharges 12.1 and the topic-specific obligations spread across Requirements 1 to 11.

## 2. The question the Chair asked

Voss asked why fourteen policies rather than one.

Bhatt's answer, minuted:

> "Because 12.1.1 does not oblige us to *write* a policy. It obliges us to establish, publish, maintain and **disseminate** one. A single ninety-page document satisfies the first three and fails the fourth. Nobody in a store reads ninety pages. Fourteen documents, each owned by somebody who can answer questions about it, is the version that reaches people."

## 3. Dissemination, and how it was tested

Publication is not dissemination, and the Committee asked for evidence of the difference. Four mechanisms were deployed, and their **findability** was tested rather than assumed — colleagues at each site were asked to locate the policy governing a specific situation within a set time.

The results were reported unrounded, including the ones that were not flattering. The weakest population was the one the Committee expected: colleagues furthest from the corporate network.

## 4. What the review of role acknowledgement found

**12.1.3** obliges all personnel to acknowledge understanding and acceptance. Acknowledgement was tested by interview rather than by counting clicks — **46 people asked what their own responsibilities were under the policy; 42 answered correctly.**

Delgado (Internal Audit) noted that a 100% click-through rate and a 91% comprehension rate are not the same measurement, and that the second is the one worth reporting.

## 5. Determination

The policy suite is **approved**, with the annual 12.1.2 review scheduled and each topic-specific policy assigned a named owner who is expected to be able to answer questions about it without reference to the document.
