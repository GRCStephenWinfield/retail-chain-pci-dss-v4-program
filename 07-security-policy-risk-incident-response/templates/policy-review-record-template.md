# Template — Annual Policy Review Record

> **12.1.2**: the information security policy is reviewed at least once every 12 months and **updated as needed** to reflect changes to business objectives or to the risk environment.
> A review that changes nothing is a valid outcome — but it must be a *conclusion*, not a default.
