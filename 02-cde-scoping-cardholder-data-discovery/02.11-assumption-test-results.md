## Purpose

01.08 wrote down twelve assumptions and sixteen questions, and said in terms that the assumptions were stated so they could be disproved. This document is the accounting.

It answers **all sixteen questions Q-01 to Q-16** and records the disposition of **all twelve assumptions A-01 to A-12**. It exists to prove that Phase 01's hypothesis was actually tested rather than assumed correct, and the proof it offers is not that most assumptions survived. It is that **two did not**, that the register says so permanently, that a risk went **up** as a result, and that the reasoning behind every disposition is written down in a form a hostile reader can attack.

It is deliberately unsentimental. A document of this kind that reports only confirmations is a document that was written before the testing.

## 1. The Disposition Taxonomy

Four dispositions are available, and they are not interchangeable.

| Disposition | Meaning | Effect on the register |
|---|---|---|
| **Confirmed** | Tested against E1 or E2 evidence; the evidence supports the assumption as written | Recorded as confirmed, with the conditions on which the confirmation depends |
| **Confirmed with qualification** | The assumption holds, but not for the reason believed, or not everywhere, or not to the strength the wording implies | Recorded as confirmed **with the qualification stated in the register entry**, not in a footnote. A qualification that is not carried forward is a disproof in slow motion |
| **Disproved** | Evidence contradicts the assumption. The belief was wrong | Recorded permanently as disproved. **Not reinstated on remediation.** The associated risk goes up |
| **Restated** | The assumption was not independently testable as written and has been reformulated into claims that are | The original is retired; the replacements enter the register with their own verification methods |

The rule the programme operates under, carried forward from earlier engagements and reaffirmed by the Steering Committee, governs the third row: **when testing disproves an assumption, the associated risk goes up, not down.** A control believed to be working and not working was never protecting anything. Discovering that changes the record, not the reality.

### 2.2 The questions whose answers were not what was expected

**Q-05 and Q-06** produced the two disproofs and are worked in full in 02.03. What is worth adding here is that they failed for **different reasons** and the difference is the transferable part. Q-05's assumption reasoned from a control to a state of the world, and the control was newer than the world. Q-06's assumption described a process accurately and treated that as a description of the data the process holds. The first error is about **time**; the second is about **residue**. Both are available to any organisation, and neither is a failure of diligence — the people who wrote A-04 and A-07 knew their business.

**Q-10 is the question this phase could not fully answer**, and saying so is the most important line in the document. Configuration evidence showed guest wireless separated from store back-office and store back-office separated from corporate. Configuration evidence is **E2**. Under the standard applied in 02.01 §5, E2 can support an exclusion where coverage and completeness are evidenced, and coverage was evidenced across all 482 sites. What E2 cannot do is prove that the configuration produces the isolation it describes, because a rule set is a statement of intent executed by devices that may be misconfigured, mis-cabled or bypassed. **Requirement 11.4.5 exists because configuration is not proof.** The answer recorded in February was "separated by configuration, not yet proved by test," and the answer given by the test in May was different. §7 says what it was.

**Q-11 produced the observation with the widest application.** Six of 137 systems recorded as decommissioned were still powered, reachable or holding retained storage — a 4.4% error rate in a database the organisation used to decide what to scan. Three of the six were AWS instances in the **stopped** state with EBS volumes retained: no compute charge, so no cost alert; filtered out of operational dashboards by default; volumes persisting with contents intact. One held 1,143 PAN instances.

**Q-13 is the only question whose answer nobody expected to be interesting.** Merchant ID reconciliation with Cardinal Merchant Bank enumerated nine merchant identifiers against Marketa's own records. Eight matched live channels. The ninth was a dormant identifier established in 2019 for an outlet-clearance pop-up programme, with no transaction activity since November 2019 and no associated system, terminal or acceptance path. It had remained open at the acquirer for six years because closing a merchant ID is nobody's task when a programme quietly ends. It was closed on **2026-02-19**. No account data was associated with it and no acceptance path existed. The finding is trivial and is reported because **an open merchant ID is an acceptance channel with no owner**, and the reason a scope analysis asks Q-13 is that the channel nobody knows about is the one with no controls.

It is what happens when the qualifications are written down instead of being absorbed into a summary.

## 4. The Two Disproofs, and What Follows

### 4.1 A-04 — no account data in the call-recording estate

**Disproved 2026-02-03.** Approximately 11,400 recordings held audible primary account numbers for between three and seven years, on a platform whose access group was wider than the two-person investigation team that later reviewed them. Detected by an audio pipeline built specifically because 01.08 §3 listed historical data as a blind spot. Remediated by 2026-03-27 with a deletion manifest; retention reduced from seven years to thirteen months under a **12.3.1** targeted risk analysis.

**The reasoning error:** the assumption reasoned from a control to a state of the world, and the control was newer than the world. DTMF suppression protects recordings made after it was deployed. Cadence's own position, recorded in the TPSP register in Phase 01 **before any of this was found**, is that the service is forward-only. The organisation held the correct fact and drew the wrong conclusion from it, which is a more uncomfortable finding than not knowing.

**Consequences flowing to Phase 03 and beyond:** risk **R-31** in the baseline register; procedure **P-1** with its standing rule that any change to a capture-affecting control triggers a retrospective review of what was captured before; condition **MOTO-C7** with annual audio discovery; the thirteen-month retention rule enforced at CT-60.

### 4.2 A-07 — dispute and chargeback processes hold no full PAN

**Disproved 2026-01-28**, five days into the exercise, by the share crawler on its first pass. 2,187 scanned issuer dispute packets, retrieval requests and signed sales drafts bearing full PAN, cardholder name and expiry, on an unencrypted volume, accessible to a 63-member inherited group of whom 22 had no dispute-handling role. Plus 33 copies in retention-locked backup sets at Ashburn that could not be surgically deleted and were tracked to natural expiry on **2026-05-31**.

**The reasoning error:** the assumption described the process as it currently runs and treated that as a description of the data the process holds. Every word of A-07 was true of the 2021-onward process. It was false about the estate, because a process that changes leaves its previous outputs behind and nobody had been given the job of deciding what happened to them.

**Consequences flowing forward:** procedure **P-2** requiring a named individual owner and explicit retention rule for every unstructured repository, with quarterly unowned-repository discovery; the immutable-backup exception pattern as a pre-approved handling route; the redesign of dispute handling into CDE-03 and CDE-06, where images are rendered in session and cannot be saved; and a data-disposition step in the definition of done for every migration project.

### 4.3 What the disproofs did not do

They did not stop being disproofs. Both are recorded in the register as **disproved and not reinstated**, permanently, and both will still be recorded that way when the controls that replaced them have run clean for years.

The distinction is about what a statement licenses. Remediation licenses a statement about the data: there is no longer account data in those locations, and here is the evidence. Reinstating an assumption would license a statement about the **reliability of the organisation's beliefs about itself** — specifically, that a deployed control describes the state of the environment. That belief was wrong. Eleven thousand four hundred deletions do not make it right.

## 5. A-05 — Restated Rather Than Counted as a Third Disproof

> **A-05:** *Marketa stores only Truvance-issued tokens and truncated PAN; no full PAN exists at rest anywhere in the estate, including email and collaboration platforms.*

All four findings contradict it. The register nonetheless records **two** disproofs, not three, and the reasoning — stated in 02.03 §7.3 so it could be argued with — is that **A-05 is an umbrella restatement of the programme's overall claim rather than an independent testable mechanism.** Every instance contradicting it is an instance of A-04 failing, or of A-07 failing, or of a data-hygiene lapse that A-05 did not attempt to model and that no control was ever asserted to prevent. Counting it as a third disproof would count the same evidence twice and would overstate the number of *mechanisms* that failed, which is what the register exists to track.

A-05 has been retired and replaced by three claims, each independently testable, each with its own verification method and owner. They enter the register for Phase 03 onward.

| Replacement | Claim | Verification method | Frequency | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A-05a** | No full PAN exists in the corporate CDE beyond what flows F-04, F-05 and F-06 specify, and none comes to rest | Discovery scanning of the 8 CDE components, including during an active settlement window | Quarterly | Marcus Hale |
| **A-05b** | No full PAN exists in the messaging and collaboration estate | **Continuous DLP telemetry** — inbound quarantine and outbound block — plus quarterly archive search | Continuous, with quarterly assurance | Marcus Hale |
| **A-05c** | No full PAN exists in unstructured repositories — shares, sites, document stores | Quarterly discovery over all repositories, including the unowned-repository sweep under procedure P-2 | Quarterly | Marcus Hale |

The distinction between an umbrella claim and a testable one is not cosmetic. **A-05 could not have been verified by any single act**, which is why it survived for years as a belief. A-05a, A-05b and A-05c each name a population, a method and a cadence, and each can be shown to have been performed or not performed.

A reviewer who thinks this is a distinction without a difference is invited to say so at the next Steering Committee. The reasoning is on the record specifically so that it can be challenged rather than discovered by inference.

| Raised | **2026-02-05**, two days after PAN-01 was confirmed |
| Score | **Likelihood 4 × Impact 4 = 16 — High** under the programme's scoring model |

The temptation to do the opposite should be named because every incentive in a compliance programme points at it. Reporting a finding and its remediation in the same breath, netting them off, and showing a risk that was briefly elevated and is now closed reads better than this does. It is also a lie of composition, because it presents the **discovery** as the risk event when the discovery was the mitigation. The exposure was identical on 2026-02-02 and on 2026-02-04. What changed on 2026-02-03 was Marketa's knowledge of it, and a risk register exists to record what the organisation knows.

R-31 is one of only **two risks in the entire 44-risk register raised on evidence rather than identified in analysis.** The other is R-14, raised when the first segmentation penetration test failed. Both entries are in Phase 03's baseline.

## 7. What Phase 02 Could Not Answer

Two questions were answered with evidence that the programme itself graded as insufficient, and both are handed to Phase 03 in that state rather than dressed up.

| Question | What Phase 02 established | What it could not establish | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Q-10 / A-09** — segmentation | Configuration evidence across all 482 sites, all nine zones, 11,847 rules analysed and a reachability computation over the whole model. **E2** | That the configuration produces the isolation it describes. Only a test does that, and the test had not been run | **11.4.5** penetration test by Ironwood Security Labs — Phase 03 |
| **Q-14 / A-11** — provider validation over time | Current, valid AOCs and a current Council listing for every provider, reconciled against the specific services consumed | That they remain valid through the compliance year. The Verition P2PE Attestation of Validation dated 2025-09-12 requires renewal inside it | Quarterly TPSP review; conditions P2PE-C1, ECOM-C7, MOTO-C8, TOK-C4 |

On the first row, the phase's own position, written in February and reproduced unaltered:

> Segmentation has been verified by configuration analysis and by reachability computation. Neither is a test. Requirement **11.4.5** requires the segmentation controls to be tested at least annually and after any change to them, and the reason the requirement is written that way is that configuration review has a systematic blind spot: it can only find the errors that are visible in the artefacts it reads. It cannot find a mis-cabled trunk, a device that does not enforce what its configuration says, or a path that exists because two correctly configured devices interact in a way neither one's configuration describes.

**The test was run in May 2026 and it failed.** One path proved segmentation was not effective — a store back-office VLAN reachable from the guest Wi-Fi VLAN through a misconfigured trunk. Risk **R-14** was raised. Remediation and a clean re-test followed in June. The finding is Phase 03's to report in full, and it is stated here because this document's purpose is to record what the assumptions were worth, and **A-09 was worth exactly as much as the evidence behind it: not enough.**

## 8. What Was Learned That Nobody Asked About

The sixteen questions were the transfer mechanism. They were not the whole yield. Eight observations came out of the exercise that no question was written to elicit, and several are more useful than the answers.

**The CMDB is not an inventory.** Reconciliation against three independent sources added **19 systems** the CMDB had never held. None held account data and none entered scope — and the finding is not the 19 systems, it is that the database supplying the tags that produced the 604 was not a complete list of the estate it was being used to scope. That is a **12.5.1** observation, and it is why the assessed register reconciles against four sources monthly rather than one source annually.

**The connected-to list was 70% right and nobody could have said which 70%.** Sixty-three components then, sixty-three now, with nineteen substituted. A register accurate in total and wrong about a third of its contents passes every headline review and fails the first walkthrough. Totals are the least informative property of an inventory.

**Business-purpose labels stop network paths from being examined.** "Patching," "backup," "monitoring" and "replication" were the four labels under which unexamined boundary crossings lived. Finding **RCH-03** — a functioning egress path from the cardholder data environment to an internet proxy — survived years of review because it was categorised as patching, and patching is a thing done *to* the CDE rather than a thing the CDE does.

**Second-order paths produced ten of the sixty-three.** Out-of-scope criterion 5 — unrestricted access to a connected-to system such that CDE security could be affected through it — is the criterion routinely skipped, and it is the one that produced the secrets vault, the certificate authority, the cloud organisation control plane and the infrastructure-as-code pipeline. Walking one hop out from the CDE finds the systems everybody already knows about.

**In the cloud, identity is reachability.** A principal who can modify a security group has reachability to everything that group protects, from anywhere they can authenticate. Fourteen principals could alter the Z2 CDE VPC's network configuration at the start of the analysis. A reachability analysis that models only packets will miss every one of them.

**Luhn is not a card algorithm, and a retailer pays for that fact.** Loyalty member IDs, closed-loop gift cards and IMEIs are all sixteen-digit and Luhn-valid, by design and for the same reason. 2,690 adjudicated false positives came from six identifiable generators. The general rule the exercise produced: **suppression by pattern traced to a published specification is auditable; suppression by tuning is not.**

**Seventy-two published test PANs were sitting in production code, fixtures and documentation.** Not cardholder data, not a scope finding, and a clear code-hygiene signal that was raised separately rather than being suppressed silently. A test PAN in a production configuration file means something about how that file got there.

**The compliant path has to be the fast path.** Finding PAN-04 was created by a competent store manager who wanted 34 customers' special orders processed before a seasonal cut-off and had no compliant route that was as quick as the non-compliant one. The awareness programme had told colleagues what not to do without telling them what to do instead, which produces improvisation, and improvisation by a capable person under time pressure produces a spreadsheet. Store special-order handling now issues a Truvance-hosted payment link, which is both compliant and faster than collating a spreadsheet. **A control that is slower than the workaround is a control with a countdown on it**, and a seasonal peak with 6,800 temporary colleagues under **CON-09** is when the countdown expires.
