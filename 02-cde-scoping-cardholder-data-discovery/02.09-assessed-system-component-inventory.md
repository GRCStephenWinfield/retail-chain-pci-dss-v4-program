## Purpose

This is the register. Requirement **12.5.1** obliges an inventory of system components in scope for PCI DSS, including a description of function or use. This document is that inventory, together with the process that keeps it true between the determination in February and fieldwork in November.

**71 assessed system components: 8 CDE and 63 connected-to or security-impacting.** The eight are presented individually because each carries a distinct account-data relationship that an assessor will want to walk. The sixty-three are presented by functional group, with the per-component detail held in 02.08 and in the register in `trackers/`.

The register is the artefact everything downstream depends on. Vulnerability scanning targets it. Log coverage is measured against it. Penetration test scoping derives from it. The assessor samples from it. A component missing from this register is a component that is not scanned, not logged, not patched to the CDE standard and not tested — which is why the reconciliation process in §7 matters at least as much as the list itself.

## 3. The 8 CDE System Components

Six sit in **Z1**, the Columbus data centre CDE core, on dedicated physical hardware in dedicated cabinets. Two sit in **Z2**, a dedicated AWS VPC in a dedicated account, us-east-1 with a us-west-2 standby.

Before the list, the account-data position needs stating precisely, because it is unusual and an assessor will test it hard. **No account data comes to rest in Marketa's CDE.** Discovery in 02.02 §9.2 found none, and the architecture does not provide for any. The CDE exists because three flows **process or transmit** account data through it — F-04, F-05 and F-06 in 02.07 §3.2 — and because the remaining components share the network segment with the ones that do. That is limb 1 and limb 2 of the CDE test in 02.01 §3, and it is a complete basis; the standard does not require storage for a component to be in the CDE.

## 6. Sampling Implications

The standard permits an assessor to sample system components where the entity operates standardised, centrally managed processes. A 71-component register changes what sampling is for.

| Population | Sampled by the assessor? | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| The 8 CDE components | **No** | Eight is not a population to sample. Every one is examined |
| The 63 connected-to components | Partially | Sampling within homogeneous groups — three domain controllers, two jump hosts, two scanners — with the justification documented. Singleton components are examined individually |
| 482 stores / 1,914 lanes | Yes, stratified | The design in 02.04 §5, carried into fieldwork |
| 310 call-centre agents | Yes | Observation-based sampling across agent groups and shift patterns |
| 6 payment page templates / 38 scripts | **No** | The whole population, every template, every script |

The observation the programme records: **scope reduction converts a sampling problem into a census problem, and a census is easier to defend.** With 604 components an assessor samples, the entity argues about the sample, and the sample's representativeness becomes a finding risk. With 71 the assessor looks at nearly all of them. That is more scrutiny per component and less argument about coverage, and on balance it is the better trade.

## 7. Maintaining the Inventory — Requirement 12.5.1

A register accurate in February and unmaintained in November is worse than no register, because it is a document the organisation believes.

| Mechanism | Frequency | Owner | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Monthly reconciliation** | Monthly, on the fifth working day | Marcus Hale | Register reconciled against the CMDB, the AWS inventory APIs, Active Directory computer objects and the vulnerability scanner's discovered-asset list. **Four sources, because the CMDB alone missed 19 systems in 02.02 §8** |
| **Scope-delta review** | On every scope-affecting change | Owen Castellanos | New integration, new third party, new acceptance method, new data flow, network or boundary change. Produces an SDR amendment or a recorded determination that scope is unaffected |
| **Condition verification cycle** | Per the condition register — continuous to annual | Condition owners | Verifies that the conditions holding each reduction still hold. A failed condition is a register event |
| **Quarterly unowned-repository discovery** | Quarterly | Marcus Hale | Procedure **P-2** from 02.03 §3; catches the class of thing that has no CMDB record at all |
| **Annual confirmation** | At least every 12 months, and on significant change | Naomi Bhatt | The **12.5.2** confirmation — 02.12 §2 |

### 7.1 How the monthly reconciliation actually runs

| Step | Test | Action on exception |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Every register entry still exists and is in the recorded location and zone | Investigate; a component that moved zone is a scope event, not an update |
| 2 | Every register entry has a current owner who is still employed and still in role | Reassign within five working days; an unowned component is the precondition for finding PAN-02 |
| 3 | Every register entry appears in the vulnerability scanner's target list and produced a scan in the period | Investigate; a component in the register and not in the scanner is a Requirement 11 gap |
| 4 | Every register entry appears in the SIEM's expected-source list and produced logs in the period | Investigate under **10.7.2**; silence is a finding, not an absence |
| 5 | Every new system appearing in any of the four inventory sources is classified within the period | Classify against the 02.01 §4 decision tree; unclassified systems are **provisionally in scope** |
| 6 | Every component removed from the register has a recorded determination and a data-destruction attestation under procedure **P-3** | Reopen if absent. **Stopped is not terminated** |

Step 6 exists because of finding PAN-03. Step 4 exists because a log source that stops reporting looks identical to a component that is behaving well.

## 9. What This Register Does Not Claim

| Claim | Position |
|---|---|
| That 71 is a permanent number | It is the number as at the determination and at the most recent reconciliation. Every reduction is conditional and several conditions are verified only annually |
| That a small register means a small compliance problem | 1,914 devices, 482 sites, 310 agents, 6 templates, 38 scripts and 5 third parties are all in the assessment and none of them is in this register |
| That the register is complete because it reconciles | It reconciles against four inventory sources. A component in none of the four would not be caught by reconciliation; it would be caught by discovery, annually, which is a slower loop and is recorded as such |
| That classification is the assessor's to make | The classification is Marketa's determination. Sable Ridge Assurance reviewed it, challenged it in three places, and recorded the assessed scope in the ROC — 02.12 §3 |
