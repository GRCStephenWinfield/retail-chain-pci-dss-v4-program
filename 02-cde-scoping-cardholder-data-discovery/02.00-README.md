## What this phase is

The most consequential phase in the portfolio. PCI DSS applies to the cardholder data environment, so **every requirement in the standard follows from where that boundary is drawn**. Phase 01 stated a hypothesis — 604 systems believed in scope — and wrote twelve assumptions so that they could be disproved. Phase 02 tests all twelve, answers all sixteen questions Phase 01 posed, and produces the defensible population the assessment will actually sample from.

## The headline, and the part that matters more

**604 → 71 assessed components, a reduction of 88.2%.**

The more useful number is buried in that arithmetic: **543 systems removed, and 10 added.** Ten systems were brought into scope that had never appeared on the original list at all. As the CISO put it at the scope review:

> "A scoping exercise that only removes systems has not tested the hypothesis. It has confirmed a preference. The ten additions are the evidence that we actually looked."

## Where the account data actually was

Discovery ran across **100% of the estate** — 1,842 of 1,842 systems, all 41 network shares, all 6 email archives, plus backup catalogue review and reconciliation of decommissioned records against live network discovery. That decision (**ADR-0007**) was made because of a question nobody could answer: *if account data is somewhere we do not expect, which sample would find it?*

It found four locations. **None of them was inside the 604.** Three were in populations no inventory covered.

| ID | Location | Volume | Why it existed |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PAN-01** | Call-centre QA recording archive | **~11,400 recordings** with audible PAN | DTMF masking was implemented **forward-only** in 2023 and never applied to what already existed |
| **PAN-02** | Legacy chargeback dispute file share | **2,187 scanned documents** + 33 backup copies | Dispute handling creates documents as a by-product of a process nobody had mapped |
| **PAN-03** | Debug log on a decommissioned e-commerce host | **1,143 PAN instances** in one 1.4 GB file | Host retired at the 2022 iframe cutover, never terminated; CMDB said it was gone |
| **PAN-04** | Store manager's emailed spreadsheet | **34 records** across 11 copies | A customer emailed a card number and someone tried to solve their problem |

All four were remediated completely. No indicator of compromise was found in any of them — with **one stated limitation** that was deliberately not closed by inference: file-server access logs for the dispute share did not exist before a 24-month window.

## Why a risk went UP after everything was fixed

**R-31 was raised to 4 × 4 = 16 (High)** on 2026-02-05 — two days after the first finding, and while remediation was already underway.

The archive is gone. The rating still rose, because it had never really been about those recordings. It encoded a **belief**: that DTMF masking had eliminated spoken account data from the call estate. That belief was wrong in a specific and instructive way — the control was forward-only. Deleting 11,400 recordings removes 11,400 recordings; it does not restore confidence in an estate that had never been searched.

**ADR-0005** makes this a standing rule, and sets an evidence-based exit: R-31 comes down when a **second full discovery run returns nothing**, due 2026-08-31 — not when the first run's findings are cleaned up.

## The discipline: every reduction is conditional

None of the three mechanisms reduces scope by existing.

| Mechanism | Removes | Only while… | What brings the systems back |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P2PE** | 482 store systems | The solution is Council-listed **and** deployed as validated **and** operated per the P2PE Instruction Manual | A non-listed device or firmware; a POI outside the approved network path; any PIM control not performed |
| **Tokenization + hosted iframe** | 31 systems | No Marketa origin collects a card field on any of the 6 templates **and** no detokenisation entitlement exists | A template collecting a card field; a fallback form; any granted entitlement |
| **DTMF masking** | 11 systems | Masking is engaged on every payment call **and** no alternate capture path exists | A bypass path; an agent capturing a PAN another way |

Three stores were found with a non-conforming POI network path on 2026-02-06. **Their reduction was suspended** until corrected on 2026-02-15. That is what "conditional" means in practice.

## The boundary people get wrong

The e-commerce checkout iframe is served from **Truvance's origin**, so card fields never touch a Marketa system and the AWS tier is out of the CDE. **The Marketa payment pages remain fully in scope for 6.4.3 and 11.6.1 anyway** — a script on the parent page can overlay, keylog or redirect regardless of where the iframe is served from. That is precisely why those future-dated requirements exist, and it is the single most misunderstood boundary in modern PCI.

## The honest position

Scope is now **evidenced rather than asserted** — for data. It is **configuration-only for segmentation**. Marketa has verified that the store estate is separated from the corporate environment by reading rule sets and routes, and configuration is not proof.

The claim is therefore bounded until the segmentation penetration test in May 2026. If that test fails, the ADR-0006 classification collapses and the store estate re-enters scope. The QSA accepted the position on exactly that basis, noting it "depends entirely on the May test."

**It will fail.** Phase 03 records what happened.
