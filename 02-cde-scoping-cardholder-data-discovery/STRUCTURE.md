## Conventions
`NN.NN` numbering in execution order; a metadata table at the head of every document; a `## Cross-References` table and footer navigation at the foot. Document IDs run `MRG-PCI-2026-201` through `-212`. ADRs are numbered continuously across the repository; Phase 02 uses **0005–0007**.

The keystone deliverables are **02.03** (the four unexpected PAN locations, worked end to end, and the risk that was raised despite full remediation), **02.09** (the definitive 71-component inventory), **02.10** (the 604 → 71 reduction with all 35 conditions and their reversal triggers), and **02.11** (the disposition of every Phase 01 assumption, two of them disproved).

The Excel trackers are **generated by parsing the narrative documents** — the CDE components from 02.09, the connected-to enumeration from 02.08, the findings from 02.02, and the questions and assumptions from 02.11 — with assertions on every count, including a check that exactly two assumptions are recorded as disproved. A workbook cannot drift from the prose it summarises.
