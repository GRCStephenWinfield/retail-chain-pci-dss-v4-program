# ADR-0035 — The Board Scorecard Keeps Its Red Measure

|---|---|
| Status | Accepted |

| Classification | Confidential — Cardholder Data Environment // Illustrative Portfolio Sample |

## Context

The board scorecard carries twelve measures. Eleven are green or amber at programme close. One is red, and it is red for a reason no control can change:

> **Incident response capability proved against a real compromise of account data.**

Marketa has handled **512 incident records** in the operating period — 5 SEV-2, 0 SEV-1, no confirmed compromise of account data. The plan exists, covers all seven elements 12.10.1 names, reaches all 71 assessed components through ten playbooks, has a twelve-person 24/7 roster proved by unannounced tests, and was exercised by somebody who wanted it to fail. **None of that is the same as having survived the event.**

The proposal at the March Audit Committee was to retire the measure as unmeasurable, on the reasonable ground that a measure nobody can turn green is not a management instrument.

## Decision

**The measure stays, and stays red.** No measure is removed from the board scorecard because it is uncomfortable, and none is redefined into something achievable.

## Consequences

The Board sees a red measure every quarter for as long as Marketa is not breached, which is the outcome everybody wants and the reporting nobody enjoys. **A scorecard that goes all-green has stopped measuring something.**

The measure is also the only one on the scorecard that reports on a **capability rather than a control**, and that distinction is the programme's most durable lesson. Eleven measures report whether something exists, operates and can be evidenced. This one reports whether it would work, and the honest answer is that nobody knows, because it has not been asked.

**R-43 — incident procedures documented but not exercised against the scenarios this estate actually produces — has not moved in any phase of this programme.** The scorecard and the register agree, which is the test of whether either is being maintained honestly.
