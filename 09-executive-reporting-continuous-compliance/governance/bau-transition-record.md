## 1. The step-down

| Body | Programme cadence | Business as usual |
|---|---|---|
| PCI Steering Committee | Monthly, chaired by the CFO | **PCI Governance Forum — quarterly**, same chair |
| PCI Working Group | Weekly | **Fortnightly** |
| QSA checkpoint | Monthly from 2026-03 | **Quarterly** |
| TPSP review | Quarterly | **Quarterly — unchanged** |
| Audit Committee | Quarterly | **Quarterly — unchanged** |
| Store readiness call | Monthly | **Quarterly, folded into the operations cycle** |

**Two cadences deliberately did not reduce.** The TPSP review stayed quarterly because six providers operate most of the controls that make the scope reduction true, and the Audit Committee stayed quarterly because the body that receives an uncomfortable report should not receive it less often once the programme that produced it has closed.
