## 2. The three things the Board was asked to accept

**That the close forecast was missed and the miss is reported rather than restated.** 03.12 §6 forecast thirteen Moderate entries at close and the actual is sixteen. The forecast moved thirteen entries across a boundary the register's own scoring discipline does not permit them to cross. **The programme is reporting the prediction it published, not the one it could have written afterwards.**

**That one open item does not close because a provider said no.** Marketa asked Truvance — the provider on whom the entire e-commerce and tokenization scope reduction depends — for a contractual response-time commitment on incident-driven token resolution, and Truvance declined at renewal. **The item is not re-worded into something closable.** It becomes BAU-01 with a named owner and the next renewal date.

**That the board scorecard keeps a red measure.** The incident response capability has never been proved by the event it exists for. **ADR-0035** prevents its removal.

## 3. Internal Audit's closing note

Rosa Delgado, for the file:

> "Over eighteen months this programme raised two risks on evidence, refused two compensating controls, reported a failed ASV scan to its acquirer, published a failed segmentation test, filed a non-compliant attestation twenty days early, declined to withdraw it after the re-assessment, and has now reported missing its own close forecast by three entries. **I have no assurance concerns about the accuracy of what is being presented.**
>
> What I would say to the Board is the thing this programme has said to itself in every phase: **none of that is the same as saying the environment is secure.** It says the reporting is honest. Those are different findings, and the second is the one you can act on."
