## Reading order

Start at `09.00-README.md`. The numbered documents run in execution order: reporting and measurement (09.01–09.02), performance and cost (09.03–09.04), disposing of what the assessment left open (09.05–09.06), the lessons (09.07), the transition to business as usual and the machinery that sustains it (09.08–09.11), and the close (09.12–09.14).

**If you read only one document, read `09.12-the-final-risk-register.md`.** It reports a forecast the programme published in month seven, shows the arithmetic of why it was missed by three entries, and names all seven impact reductions in eighteen months.

## How the trackers are produced

Every workbook is **generated by parsing the narrative markdown** with assertions. The Phase 09 builder independently reconstructs all 44 register entries from the Phase 03 baseline and every recorded movement, and asserts that the position entering the phase is 0 · 24 · 20 and the position at close is **0 · 16 · 28**. It also asserts that each movement's likelihood × impact equals its stated score, that every impact reduction is labelled as one, and that the metric catalogue, the seventeen observations, the eleven open items and the forty-seven calendar obligations all reconcile to their stated totals. **A workbook cannot drift from the prose, because it is derived from it.**
