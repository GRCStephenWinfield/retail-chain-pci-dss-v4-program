# Template — Board Compliance Report

> Four pages. A board does not need 306 dispositions; it needs the status, the exceptions, the money, what could still go wrong, and **the measure that is red**.

## Page 2 — Exceptions

For each exception: the requirement, why it is not met by the defined approach, the remediation date, who owns it, and **whether a compensating control was argued and refused.** A refused compensating control is a more useful line for a board than an accepted one.

**No fine, fee or penalty amount is stated.** The consequences of non-compliance are described only as categories assessed against the acquirer and passed through under the merchant agreement.

## Page 4 — The scorecard, and the red measure

| Measure | RAG | Movement since last report | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|

**If every measure is green, the scorecard has stopped measuring something.** State which measure would be red under a stricter reading, and why it is not.

## What a board should ask, and the answer should be on the page

| Question | Where it is answered |
|---|---|

| What are we assuming that we have not tested? | Page 4 |
| Which of these numbers is a statement about a control, and which about a capability? | Page 4 |
| What stops being done next year, and who decided that? | Page 3 |
