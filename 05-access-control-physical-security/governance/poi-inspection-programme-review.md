## 2. The Q3 discrepancies

Six terminals showed tamper-evident seal discrepancies. All six were escalated under POI-8, removed from service pending resolution, and **all six were cleared** — five were unrecorded reseals following a Verition service wave in September, one was solvent damage from a cleaning product.

**The procedure was tightened anyway.** Nwosu's reasoning, minuted:

> "Nobody touched a device. But a vendor service wave that leaves six seals we cannot account for is a process gap, and next time the six might not all be benign. 'All cleared' is not the same as 'nothing to learn'."

Verition now files a service record per terminal before a technician leaves the store, and the record is reconciled against the next inspection.

## 3. What the Committee refused to change

A regional manager proposed raising the escalation threshold so that a single seal anomaly would not remove a terminal from service during trading.

**Declined.** Raising a threshold because the first findings were benign is how the next one gets missed. The cost of six unnecessary removals in a quarter was accepted as the price of the control working.

## 4. The measure that actually tests the training

Coverage measures whether inspections happened. It does not measure whether a colleague would detect anything.

Nine **unannounced tests** of repair-personnel identity verification were conducted across the year — someone in a Verition-branded shirt arriving without a work order. **Two failed**: the colleague allowed access without verifying. Both were retrained and re-tested successfully.

Bhatt recorded that this figure is more informative than the 99.56%:

> "Ninety-nine point five six tells me the inspections happened. Two out of nine tells me what the inspections are worth."

## 5. The structural position

1,914 terminals sit in public spaces, operated by roughly 29,600 colleagues with high turnover. **The exposure does not go away.** R-02 reduces on the strength of the operating regime, but the underlying condition is structural and is recorded as such in the register.
