## What this phase is

PCI Requirements **7, 8 and 9**. It contains the programme's largest single workstream (**8.4.2**), its only **customized approach** (8.3.9), one of its two **Not in Place** findings (8.6.2), and a control executed by roughly 29,600 people who are not security professionals (**9.5.1**).

## The sentence entities misread

> **8.4.2 is not "MFA for admins". It is MFA for *all* access into the cardholder data environment.**

An organisation that already had MFA on administrative and remote access under v3.2.1 — as Marketa did — can read 8.4.2 and conclude it is already compliant. It is not. The population that changes is the **ordinary user logging into a CDE system from inside the corporate network**: the disputes analyst, the settlement clerk, the refunds administrator.

At Marketa that is **36 people who had never used a second factor**, against 15 administrators who always had. The workstream touched **all 71 assessed components** across **20 access paths**, and it is the largest thing the programme built.

| | **8.4.1** | **8.4.2** | **8.4.3** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Obliges | MFA for non-console **administrative** access | **MFA for ALL access into the CDE** | MFA for **remote** network access |
| Population | Administrators | **Every user, administrative or not** | Remote users and third parties |
| Status | In force since v3.2.1 | **Future-dated; mandatory since 2025-03-31** | In force since v3.2.1 |

## The customized approach — the thing most portfolios never show

v4's genuinely new idea is the **customized approach**: meet the stated Customized Approach Objective by other means. Marketa uses it **exactly once**, at **8.3.9**, for a bounded population of **61 password-only accounts across 19 connected-to or vendor-managed components** — the residual set left over after 8.4.2's MFA removes password-only *user* access to the CDE itself.

Both defined options were examined and rejected **on the record**. Ninety-day rotation on vendor-managed accounts produces credential sprawl rather than security. And the second option — dynamic analysis of account posture with real-time access decisions — could not honestly be claimed, because **for 51 of the 61 accounts the component authenticates locally**: there is no external decision point at which a real-time determination could be made. As the Programme Manager noted, that second option is the one most entities quietly claim. Marketa declined to.

The phase delivers the full apparatus: the Objective quoted verbatim, the **12.3.2 targeted risk analysis**, an Appendix E1 **controls matrix** (CAC-1…CAC-8), and **twelve testing procedures derived by the QSA** (CTP-1…CTP-12) — by Sable Ridge, not by Marketa. Under the customized approach **the entity does not write its own test.**

And it states the price: roughly **46 additional assessor hours every year, forever**, re-argued from scratch when the assessor rotates in 2028. The CISO's answer to the CFO's question about why:

> "Because the alternative is to rotate passwords on sixty-one accounts every ninety days, tell ourselves that is security, and hope nobody asks what it achieved. If the population were six hundred rather than sixty-one I would give you a different answer, and the arithmetic is in the paper."

## The finding: 8.6.2 Not in Place

Two legacy batch integrations — `batch-icq` on CDE-05 and `gl-extract` on CDE-04 — still authenticate with **credentials embedded in configuration files**. Of 142 application and system accounts, 140 were moved to vaulted retrieval or workload identity. These two were not.

**A compensating control was argued and refused.** The credentials are readable by anyone with configuration access, and monthly rotation does not change that. Under **ADR-0004** the requirement is recorded as **Not in Place with a remediation date of 2027-01-31** — one of the two findings that makes the 2026 AOC non-compliant. **R-17 is held at High 16.**

Recording a compensating control would have produced a compliant AOC and a false position.

## The control 29,600 people execute

**9.5.1** obliges protection of POI devices from tampering and substitution: **1,914 terminals across 482 stores**, inspected quarterly under **TRA-9.5.1.2.1**.

| Quarter | Inspected | Coverage | Discrepancies | Confirmed tampering |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | 1,889 | 98.7% | 23 | **0** |
| Q2 | 1,908 | 99.7% | 9 | **0** |
| **Q3** | **1,914** | **100%** | **6** | **0** |
| Q4 | 1,911 | 99.8% | 11 | **0** |
| **Year** | **7,622 of 7,656** | **99.56%** | **49** | **0** |

**ADR-0017** designs the inspection for the shift rather than for the policy: the aid shows photographs of what *correct* looks like rather than describing what tampering looks like; serial verification is mandatory because substitution is the attack a visual check misses; photograph capture replaces the written report; and escalation requires **no judgement from the colleague** — any anomaly removes the terminal from service.

Q3's six discrepancies were **all cleared as maintenance** — five unrecorded reseals from a vendor service wave, one solvent damage. **The procedure was tightened anyway** (ADR-0018), because a service wave leaving six unaccounted seals is a process gap whether or not a device was touched. And the escalation threshold was deliberately **not** raised: raising a threshold because the first findings were benign is how the next one gets missed.

The figure that actually tests the training is not the coverage percentage. It is the **nine unannounced repair-personnel identity tests, of which two failed.** As the CISO put it: *"Ninety-nine point five six tells me the inspections happened. Two out of nine tells me what the inspections are worth."*

## Two findings about the programme, not the controls

**Nineteen orphaned accounts belonged to contractors who had never been registered in HR at all**, so no leaver event could ever have fired for them. The joiner–mover–leaver process was working correctly on a population it did not know existed.

**The Ashburn co-location was not in the third-party register**, so nobody had ever asked its operator for evidence that Marketa's Requirement 9 obligations are met in a building Marketa does not run. **ADR-0016** makes the rule explicit: physical security at a third-party facility is **evidenced, not inherited**.

## The honest position

Access control and physical security are implemented across all 71 assessed components and all 482 stores. Two structural facts remain, and neither is going away.

**R-17 stays High**, because two integrations still hold credentials in configuration and no compensating control was accepted for them. **R-02 reduces but does not disappear**, because 1,914 terminals sit in public spaces operated by a high-turnover population — the operating regime is good, the underlying condition is structural.

And MFA has **not yet operated through a peak trading season**. Five documented exceptions exist, all time-bounded, all due to close before the freeze. The September penetration test and the October–January peak are the first real examinations of any of this.
