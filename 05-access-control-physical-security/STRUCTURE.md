## Conventions
`NN.NN` numbering in execution order; a metadata table at the head of every document; a `## Cross-References` table and footer navigation at the foot. Document IDs run `MRG-PCI-2026-501` through `-513`. ADRs are numbered continuously across the repository; Phase 05 contributes **0013–0019** (0013–0015 are recorded inline in the first half's documents; 0016–0019 have their own files).

The keystone deliverables are **05.05** (MFA under 8.4.2 — the largest workstream in the programme), **05.06** (the only customized approach, delivered with the objective, the 12.3.2 analysis, the Appendix E1 controls matrix and the QSA-derived testing procedures), **05.07** (where the 8.6.2 Not in Place finding originates) and **05.09** (9.5.1 across 1,914 terminals, a control executed by roughly 29,600 store colleagues rather than by security professionals).

The Excel trackers are **generated by parsing the narrative documents** — the MFA matrix from 05.05, the controls matrix and testing procedures from 05.06, the quarterly inspection results from 05.09, and the role catalogue and traceability from 05.02 and 05.12 — with assertions verifying 20 contiguous MFA access paths, 24 roles, four inspection quarters, and that **confirmed tampering across the year totals zero**. A workbook cannot drift from the prose it summarises.
