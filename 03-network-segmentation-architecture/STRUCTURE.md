## Conventions
`NN.NN` numbering in execution order; a metadata table at the head of every document; a `## Cross-References` table and footer navigation at the foot. Document IDs run `MRG-PCI-2026-301` through `-313`. ADRs are numbered continuously across the repository; Phase 03 contributes **0008–0009** and **amends ADR-0006**.

The keystone deliverables are **03.07** (the penetration test that failed, with the attack path step by step), **03.08** (remediation, the 482-store rollout, and the re-test that passed), **03.11** (the 44-risk baseline register) and **03.12** (traceability proving no High risk is untreated).

The Excel trackers are **generated by parsing the narrative documents** — the register from 03.11, the test findings from 03.07, the zones from 03.02 and the traceability from 03.12 — with assertions that are unusually strict here: the builder verifies 44 risks with IDs R-01 to R-44 and no gaps, exactly 9 High / 21 Moderate / 14 Low, **and that every risk's likelihood × impact equals its stated score**. A workbook cannot drift from the prose, and neither can the arithmetic.
